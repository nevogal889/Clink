# Clink

Drink breaks with your people. A home-screen widget and companion app for iOS and Android: drink snaps on friends' widgets, "who joins?" flares, Bring-me-one drink runs with saved orders, and a private drink and water tracker.

## Where things are

- `CLAUDE.md`: the short operating manual. Read it first.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`: every approved product decision. Wins over everything else.
- `docs/PRD.md`: v1 product spec with acceptance criteria, and the open CEO questions (section 8).
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: tech design and build plan (Expo + native widgets, Supabase).
- `docs/DESIGN.md`: design system, drink art, screen and widget specs.
- `docs/COPY.md`: every user-facing string, the drink catalog and option sets.
- `docs/RISK_REVIEW.md`: security and legal review, with the resolution log.
- `docs/QA_PLAN.md`: test plan and device matrix.
- `docs/original-prd.md`: the founder's first PRD.
- `prototype.html`: clickable prototype of the agreed flows. Open it in a browser. Its visuals are placeholders.
