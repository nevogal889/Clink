# Build notes: art-b (drink icons)

Unit scope: the 42 drink icons of `DESIGN.md` 3.4, Icon S (24 grid) and Icon L (64 grid). Written 2026-11-15.

## What exists

```
app/assets/art/icons/
  {drinkId}.24.svg, {drinkId}.64.svg   84 files, one pair per catalog id (COPY.md 2.2 to 2.6)
  theme.json                            dark-mode colour swap for the export script
  _source/draw-icons.mjs                draws every icon in code and writes the SVGs
  _source/check-icons.mjs               validates the set (see below)
  _source/build-sheet.mjs               writes docs/art/icons-sheet.html, optional PNG
docs/art/icons-sheet.html               contact sheet: S at 24 px, S on category tint, L at 64 px, S at 3x; light and dark
```

The icons are drawn in code (CEO decision in `DECISIONS.md`): each vessel V1 to V11 is a surface of revolution built from the 0.30 rim and base ellipses, and each drink adds its liquid, layers, marker and garnish. Nothing is traced or generated by an image model. The SVG files are output; to change art, edit `draw-icons.mjs` and re-run it. The checker fails if a file was hand-edited.

## Commands

| What | Command |
|---|---|
| Redraw all icons | `node app/assets/art/icons/_source/draw-icons.mjs` |
| Check the set | `node app/assets/art/icons/_source/check-icons.mjs` |
| Contact sheet (+ PNG) | `node app/assets/art/icons/_source/build-sheet.mjs --png /tmp/sheet.png` (needs `playwright` with chromium) |

`check-icons.mjs` asserts: ids equal `packages/shared/generated/catalog.json` (42), both grids present, no extra files, `viewBox` exact, no text, rasters, filters, classes, styles or links, every colour a literal hex from the `DESIGN.md` 3.2 palette or its derived L shade tone, no gleam on S, under 12 KB per file, and files equal the generator output. Negative-tested (bad colour, `<text>`, stray file, hand edit all fail). Total size 151 KB for 84 files (budget 1.5 MB for all drink art).

## Review pass (contact sheet looked at, 24 px and 64 px, light and dark)

First render, then fixed:
- Foam dome (Cappuccino), foam spoonful (Macchiato) and cream peak (Mocha) had their closing chord stroked, so they read as lids. Now only the top contour is outlined.
- Saucers were two outlined ellipses and read as a heavy black ring. Now one silhouette with a light front-edge line.
- Chawan showed almost no dark glaze under the wide rim. Narrower and deeper; drip band and clay foot now read.
- Gaiwan (Oolong) read as a plate. Deeper bowl, smaller lid seated on the right of the rim, tilted 10°, outline-only lid edge so there is one ink rim.
- Star anise had no edge on dark; thin outline added. Mango cube too small at 24; enlarged.
- Berry cluster collided with the straw; moved right. Açaí's three dots were hidden under the rim; level lowered to 70% so the dots sit on a visible surface.
- Straws in short glasses ran to the top of the box; capped at 7.2 units above the rim.

Still weak, for Design to judge:
- **Dark mode, white bodies.** With the spec outline `#EEF2F5` on enamel and ceramic bodies, the cup and saucer merge into one white shape at 24 px (Espresso, Flat White, Macchiato, Cappuccino, Earl Grey, Hojicha). The silhouettes still differ, and the rim line and liquid carry the drink. A fix would need a spec change, e.g. a dark "cut" line between overlapping white parts in dark mode.
- **Oolong at 24 px** is the busiest icon: lid, knob, bowl and saucer in 9 px of height. It's recognisable as a lidded bowl but not crisp.
- At 24 px the rim stroke (2.0) leaves about 1 px of liquid visible inside cups. Cup drinks are told apart by vessel, dome, garnish and rim colour, which matches the rule that the name always shows next to the icon.

## Choices where the spec was silent or contradictory

- Detail stroke (ice, garnish outlines, marks) is 2/3 of the body stroke: 1.0 on S, 2.0 on L.
- L adds the inner back wall (shade tone crescent inside the rim) on opaque vessels that aren't full to the rim. It shows depth and stays inside the palette.
- Gleam: drawn as specified (7% width, 18% in, 30% to 70% height). The rule "the left wall stroke breaks where the gleam crosses it" can't apply because the gleam sits 18% inside the wall, so the wall stays unbroken. Design should drop or reword that sentence in 3.2.
- Chawan height 7.5 (spec 7) so the glaze shows under a 0.30 rim; foot rings are 0.5 to 0.9 units (spec 1/24 of height, under half a pixel at 24 px).
- Glass vessels show a 0.4-unit wall and a 1.0-unit thick glass floor below the liquid.
- Colours chosen from the palette where 3.4 names none: straws orange `#F2992E` (as in the hero); ice white at 70%; bubbles white 60% with the water bubble outline; lime wheel pith glazed ceramic `#EEF0EE`; roasted hojicha leaf cinnamon `#8A4B2A`; açaí dots banana slice; cortado milk and jasmine centre use foam and lemon; apple and watermelon seeds use bean `#4A2A1C`; strawberry seeds pith.
- Tea tag on Earl Grey (V2) and English Breakfast (V3) hangs over the front right of the rim, left of the handle, so it doesn't fight the handle.
- Carrot Ginger (V11): the ink rim line is the cap's top ellipse.
- Cortado layer order follows 3.4's "bottom / top" reading (espresso under milk), the same order as Strawberry and Mango Matcha.
